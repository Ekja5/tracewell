# Discovery interview guide

**Purpose:** test the hypothesis that current tools cover roughly 65% of needs (customer feedback, *unverified*) by reconstructing real recent work. Do not ask people to rate their software. Ask them to walk through the **last five cases that required a spreadsheet, text, phone call or manual correction**.

**Who:** 2–3 reps, 1–2 warehouse staff, hospital receiving/SPD at each pilot hospital, 1–2 finance/billing staff, the ops director. 45 minutes each. Use synthetic or de-identified case references only; **do not record patient information**.

## Opening (2 min)
"We're trying to understand where the work actually happens outside your systems. Can you pull up your texts, email or call log for the last two weeks?"

## For each of the last five cases (8 min each)

1. What was the case (procedure type, facility, day and time)? What went wrong or nearly went wrong?
2. How did you first find out? (system alert, text, call, walking into the OR)
3. Walk me through every step you took, in order. Which tools did you use? (spreadsheet, text, phone, ERP, CRM, paper)
4. **Time:** about how long did it take you? Who else spent time on it?
5. **Frequency:** how often does this kind of thing happen per week or month?
6. **Money:** did it cause a courier run, a write-off, a credit, a delayed or disputed bill, an expired or lost unit? Roughly how much?
7. **Authority:** who had the authority to resolve it (to move stock, accept a substitute plan from the surgeon's office, release a tray, issue a credit)? Did you have to wait for them?
8. What information did you wish you had at the moment you found out?
9. What would have prevented it?

## Role-specific probes

- **Reps:** last time you pulled a unit from your trunk for someone else's case. How did you know it wasn't promised elsewhere? How do you record usage today, and when?
- **Warehouse:** how do you decide what to pick first? What happens when a tray comes back short?
- **Hospital receiving/SPD:** what are your receiving deadlines and the evidence you require before releasing a loaner tray? Who on your side may record release? How do you report a missing instrument to the distributor?
- **Finance:** for your three oldest open cases, what is holding each one up? How do PO mismatches, opened-unused items and credits get resolved today?

## Capture sheet (one row per reconstructed case)

| Role | Case ref (synthetic) | Issue type | How discovered | Tools used | Minutes spent (all people) | Frequency | $ impact (est.) | Who had authority | Wait time for authority | Would Tracewell have changed it? (interviewer's note, verify later) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Close by asking: "If you could fix only one of these, which one and why?" Aggregate by issue type, and treat the 65% figure as a hypothesis to confirm or reject.

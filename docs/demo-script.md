# Distributor demo script (second pass, about 10 minutes)

**Lead problem:** it is 4 p.m. and tomorrow's surgeries changed. Who owns each fix, and how much time is left? Everything shown is synthetic, and integrations are simulated.

**Setup:** `DEMO_TIME=16:00 npm start -- --reset`. The yellow banner should read *Demo clock: … 4:00 PM CST*. Use the development-only account buttons on the login page, one browser profile per role. Phone steps work best in a real phone browser or in desktop dev tools with iPhone emulation.

| Min | Who | Do this | Say / point out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Ops manager (Morgan Hale) | Sign in. The readiness board sorts the most urgent case first. | **SYN-1002** at Prairie Valley is **Blocked** and **ESCALATED**. Prairie Valley receiving closes at **3:00 PM Eastern**, so the implants had to be there by then. The board did that math in the hospital's time zone. Every line ends with *"Morgan Hale owns the follow-up"*: a person, not a department. |
| 1:00 | Scheduler (Pat Rivera) | Open **SYN-1001** (St. Brigid, 7:30 AM tomorrow). Under *Schedule changes*, set the start to **5:30 AM** in *facility local time*, reason "OR block moved", then **Move case**. | No time-zone arithmetic for the scheduler. |
| 2:00 | Ops | Back on the board. | SYN-1001's tray now **must leave by 4:35 PM**: **35 minutes left**, owned by **Sam Okafor** (warehouse). The insert's deadline moved to St. Brigid's **8:00 PM receiving close** (*receiving closes at 8:00 PM*). The stored shipment cutoff and the pick queue changed with it. Sam received one updated alert (*"deadline… earlier by 120 min"*), not five new ones. |
| 3:00 | Ops | On the insert line (owned by rep Riley), click **Add note**: "Jordan's trunk has one". On a SYN-1002 line, click **Assign** and choose Jordan Ames. Open **Shift handoff**. | A coordinator coming on shift sees what is open, who has the next move, and every step already tried, without calling anyone. |
| 4:00 | Warehouse (Sam) | **Alerts** shows the grouped alert. On the board, **Acknowledge**, then **Pick, pack and dispatch** from the pick queue (it is ordered by the *new* cutoff). | Acknowledged is not resolved: the line reads *"Acknowledged by Sam Okafor"* until the records show the tray moved. Then the issue hands itself to the courier and receiving stages. |
| 5:00 | Rep Riley (phone) | **Item finder**, type `NP-INS-4-10`. | One usable unit (Jordan's trunk). Each card says *why not* for the others: reserved for SYN-1003, expired, on hold, hospital-owned. It also shows how fresh the evidence is: *"Counted here 5 days ago"* vs *"Never physically scanned (opening import)"*. Pick SYN-1001 in the case box to rank sources against St. Brigid's cutoff, then reserve. |
| 5:45 | Optional: Rep Jordan | Try to reserve the same unit for another case. | The second person is told who got it, for which case and when, and is shown the next eligible unit. The list refreshes. |
| 6:15 | St. Brigid receiving | Receive the tray and verify it with the femoral sizer at **0**. | The board says *"Tray TRY-K-002 is missing 1 required instrument… Morgan Hale owns the follow-up."* Ops **Acknowledges** and clicks **Send exact replacement from warehouse**. After the replacement is received and the tray re-verified, the same issue moves to *awaiting hospital release*, now owned by **Robin Shah (SPD)**. When SPD records release, the issue **closes itself** and its alerts close. Only hospital staff can record release; the software does not infer sterility. |
| 7:30 | Rep Riley (phone) | **My cases** → **Close case**. Tap **Implanted** once, then scan three labels in a row (scanner or simulator). Scan the femoral label again. Then tap **Correct this line** on one entry. | One scan per item, with a clear confirmation each time (*Saved on phone* / *Confirmed by server*). The rescan is caught: *"Already recorded… not saved again."* Correcting a line reverses the stock movement and voids the draft charge. Two scans of a lot-controlled screw count as two units. |
| 8:30 | Finance (Avery) | **Billing line queue** → `cannot price` → SYN-0903 NP-TIB-4. Enter a price and reason, then **Approve charge**. Open the case, **Generate billing packet**, **Send to ERP** (simulated), then record a PO that leaves NP-TIB-4 unticked. | Every held line shows amount, reason, owner, age and the missing evidence. The price change is kept with its approver. The packet shows **PO partial** and exactly what is **outstanding**; click any total to see its records. Issue a credit: money changes, stock does not (the implant stays consumed). |
| 9:30 | St. Brigid receiving | Search `SYN-1002`; open Alerts and Shipments. | Nothing from Prairie Valley appears, including Prairie Valley's case reference on a tray St. Brigid hands to the courier. |

The original first-build story (8:00 AM start: rescue, tray, offline closeout, packet, recall) still runs end to end as `tests/e2e/demo-story.spec.ts`, and the steps in this script are covered by `tests/e2e/second-pass.spec.ts` and `tests/integration/second-pass.test.ts`.

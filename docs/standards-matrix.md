# Standards applicability matrix

**Intended use.** Tracewell is logistics, inventory, custody and billing support software for distributors, manufacturer field teams and hospital partners. It does not diagnose, treat or inform clinical decisions, it does not determine sterility, and it does not issue clinical clearance. This matrix is an engineering view to support legal and regulatory review. It is **not** a regulatory determination. A passing test suite is evidence of tested behavior, not certification or compliance.

Legend: **SW** = software control implemented here · **Tested** = covered by automated tests in this repo · **Customer** = customer procedure or decision · **Validation** = validation evidence the customer or manufacturer must produce · **Contract** = agreement needed · **Deploy** = deployment or operations obligation.

| Standard / regulation | Applicability to this product | What the software does (SW / Tested) | What remains outside the software |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FDA UDI rule (21 CFR Part 801 Subpart B, Part 830) and GUDID** | Labelers (manufacturers) must label devices with a UDI and submit DI records to GUDID. A distributor's software is a **user** of UDI data, not a labeler. | SW: captures the raw label; parses the DI (GTIN), lot, serial and expiry; keeps unit DIs separate from package DIs; never derives a PI from catalog data. Tested: parser vectors, package-scan behavior, raw scan preserved. | Customer: decide whether to cross-check DIs against GUDID (no GUDID integration is built; the public GUDID API could be evaluated later). Labeling accuracy remains the manufacturer's responsibility. |
| **GS1 General Specifications (AIs 01, 10, 17, 21, 11, 30/37; FNC1; GS1 DataMatrix)** | Most orthopedic implant labels use GS1. | SW: element-string parser with check-digit and date validation and the GS1 century rule; `]d2` and FNC1/GS handling; wedge placeholders. Tested: unit tests plus a differential check against the independent `gs1-barcode-parser-mod` library. | Validation: test with real scanners and real customer labels before production (see known limitations). |
| **HIBCC (HIBC LIC/PAS)** | Some manufacturers label with HIBCC. | SW: **detects** HIBCC and routes it to reviewed manual entry, with no guessing. Tested. | No verified HIBCC parser is included. Adding one requires a validated library and test labels. |
| **QMSR (21 CFR Part 820, incorporating ISO 13485:2016, effective Feb 2, 2026)** | Applies to **manufacturers**. It is relevant only if a manufacturer uses Tracewell inside its quality system (for example, for recall execution records or complaint-related traceability). Distributors are generally not subject to Part 820, but may support manufacturer recalls under 21 CFR Part 806/7. | SW: recall entry with second-person verification (a DB constraint plus a server check); holds; tasks; acknowledgments; quarantine and disposition movements; closure evidence; append-only audit trail. Tested: recall-while-offline flow, verification by a different user. | Customer: decide intended use within its QMS, run supplier controls for this software, and maintain its own recall procedures. Validation: the manufacturer validates the software for its QMS use. |
| **FDA Computer Software Assurance (final guidance, 2025) for production and quality-system software** | Applies only where a manufacturer uses this software as part of production or its quality system. | Provides risk-relevant evidence: automated tests named after the acceptance criteria, deterministic seeds, and a test report stating what ran. | Validation: the manufacturer performs a risk-based assurance assessment (intended use, risk, testing approach, records). The pilot test suite can serve as one input but is not an assurance record. |
| **21 CFR Part 11 (electronic records and signatures)** | Applies to records that a predicate rule requires (for example, manufacturer recall or complaint records) when kept electronically. Most distributor logistics records are not predicate-rule records. | SW: unique user accounts; hashed passwords; session expiry; role-based permissions; append-only audit log with actor, occurred and recorded times; append-only ledger and verification records; reasons required for moves, cancellations, reviews and credits; second-person verification for recalls and count approvals. Tested: append-only triggers, second-person checks. | **Not implemented:** Part 11 electronic signatures (signature manifestation, re-authentication at signing, signature/record linking), formal record retention or export validation, and documented system validation. Customer: SOPs, training, account administration. Deploy: time synchronization, backups. |
| **HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules** | Applies only if the deployment creates, receives or maintains ePHI on behalf of a covered entity (the distributor would then be a business associate). The pilot is designed **not** to hold ePHI: synthetic opaque case references; no patient names, MRNs, DOBs or clinical notes. | SW: no patient fields exist in the schema; least privilege by tenant, facility and territory; access logging; no sensitive data in application logs; offline data limited to the assigned rep and case and cleared at sign-out. Tested: cross-tenant and cross-facility isolation. | Before any patient-linked data: privacy review, data-flow diagram, BAA with each covered entity (and subprocessors), risk analysis (§164.308(a)(1)), encryption at rest and in transit (Deploy), audit-review procedures, breach notification procedures. An opaque real-world case ID can still be linkable. Treat it as potentially identifying until reviewed. |
| **State laws, hospital vendor credentialing, GPO/IDN contracts** | Likely relevant to deployments. | Out of scope for software controls. | Customer and contract work. |

References checked by name only; confirm current text with regulatory counsel: 21 CFR 801/830 (UDI), 21 CFR 820 (QMSR, effective 2026-02-02), 21 CFR 806 and Part 7 (corrections, removals, recalls), 21 CFR Part 11, 45 CFR Parts 160/164 (HIPAA), FDA guidance "Computer Software Assurance for Production and Quality System Software".
